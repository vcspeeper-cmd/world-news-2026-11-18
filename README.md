A single 1024x1024 editorial poster summing up Wednesday 18 November 2026.

The lead panel covers the first three-way summit of Trump, Xi and Putin in
Beijing -- a trade truce, nuclear-arms talks and the two Americans held in
China. The second panel covers the widening Iran war in the Gulf. Three
briefs cover the Nvidia-led sell-off and the Fed's hike warning, the declared
super El Nino, and Europe's split over Rwanda deportation plans. A ticker
carries four more one-line stories.

The poster is drawn deterministically with Pillow -- no randomness, no clocks,
no network -- so every run produces byte-identical output. `main.py` is a
standard-library-only entry point that copies the committed PNG to
`/output/entry.png`.
